The traditions of Morocco, rediscovered at an unhurried pace.

Dar Ambar is a small dining room inside the gardens of Le Jardin Nakhil. Seasonal Moroccan cooking, plated with care, in a room lit mostly by lanterns. Nobody raises their voice here. That is the point.

A room carved by hand.

Chiselled plaster, cedar mashrabiya and lantern light. Every surface was made slowly, so the evening can be too.

The flavours of the season.

The carte follows the market, so it changes through the year. These are a few of the plates that have come to the table lately.
